“GO EAGLES”

Category: Typography

Year: 2024

A bold typographic illustration created for the Miss Kanyin project. This piece explores the intersection of hand-lettering and visual storytelling, using dramatic red tones against a deep black background to command attention and evoke energy.

The artwork draws on the aesthetic of classic Nigerian street culture, combining bold lettering with expressive visual language to capture the personality and spirit of the subject.

Each element was carefully crafted to reflect the energy and movement of the music, creating a graphic language that resonates with the vibrancy and spirit of contemporary African music.

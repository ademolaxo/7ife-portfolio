“Nollywood”

Category: Illustration

Year: 2024

My work on My Father’s Shadow involved creating a series of painted portraits of each major character in the film — a story set over a single day in Lagos, 1993, during the politically charged backdrop of the presidential election. 

Each piece was developed to capture the emotional depth, tension, and inner world of the characters, translating their personalities and relationships into visual form.

I approached the portraits with a focus on mood, texture, and expression, using light and color to reflect the subtle psychological layers of the story. The work emphasizes the contrasts between vulnerability and strength, absence and presence — echoing the narrative while remaining firmly rooted in the painterly aesthetic I explored throughout the series.

The result is a cohesive visual that follows the movie poster, and offers the viewers an intimate window into the characters' inner lives, while complementing the world of the film
